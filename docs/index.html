Runs on your side of the screen

Simpld is one window around Simply, an assistant that answers from model weights stored on your own hardware. Here is exactly what that buys you.

Everything local
Your code, files and questions never leave the machine. What Simply reads, only Simply reads.
Switchable offline
Once the weights are on disk the internet can be switched off entirely. The router can die mid-question and nothing about your workflow changes.
One GPU, any vendor
Simpld loads the model you choose onto your own graphics card: CUDA on NVIDIA, Vulkan on AMD and Intel, Metal on Apple Silicon.
Sized to your machine
The built-in library runs several tiers, from small files that lift on almost anything to full-size weights for capable GPUs. Download only what your hardware can carry.
Free, forever
No account, no subscription, no telemetry. Open source, so you can read every line while your answers generate.

Model weights are not bundled. The app starts with a mock model and downloads real weights once per machine, from inside the app.
